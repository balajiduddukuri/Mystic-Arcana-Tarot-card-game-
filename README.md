## 🌌 Understanding the Tarot System

The traditional 78-card Tarot deck is divided into two primary sections:

* **The Major Arcana (22 Cards):** Represents major spiritual milestones, karmic lessons, archetypal energies, and soul growth throughout the "Fool's Journey."
* **The Minor Arcana (56 Cards):** Represents daily life events, human experiences, thoughts, feelings, and actions. It is split into four elemental suits of 14 cards each (Ace through 10, plus four Court Cards: Page, Knight, Queen, King):
  * **Wands (Fire):** Passion, inspiration, ambition, drive, and spiritual energy.
  * **Cups (Water):** Emotions, love, intuition, connections, and creativity.
  * **Swords (Air):** Intellect, logic, communication, conflict, and truth.
  * **Pentacles (Earth):** Finances, material wealth, physical body, security, and career foundation.

## 🃏 Tarot Spreads & Reading Methodologies

### 1. The Single Card Arcana (Card of the Day)
* **Purpose:** Quick daily celestial compass, focus intent, or single question clarity.
* **Layout:** A single card drawn from the center of the deck.

### 2. Three-Card Past / Present / Future Spread
* **Purpose:** Provides a linear temporal perspective on any life situation.
  1. **Card 1 (Left):** Past influences and root causes.
  2. **Card 2 (Center):** Present energy, current crossroad.
  3. **Card 3 (Right):** Potential future outcome and advice.

### 3. The 5-Card Cross Guidance Spread
* **Purpose:** Deeper situation analysis covering subconscious roots and external environments.
  1. **Center:** Current Core Situation.
  2. **Left:** Underlying Challenge / Obstacle.
  3. **Right:** Higher Spiritual Advice / Action.
  4. **Bottom:** Subconscious Root / Hidden Energy.
  5. **Top:** Potential Future Outcome.
